## Design Context

### Users
Saudi youth (ages 12–18) and their parents, plus school administrators (activities coordinators, principals) looking for curriculum-aligned experiential programs. Arabic-first audience with English as a parallel locale. Users browse on mobile in Arabic or desktop in English. Parents are weighing trust and safety; teens want adventure and peer connection; schools want measurable educational outcomes.

### Brand Personality
Bold, trustworthy, aspirational. Three words: **daring · purposeful · grounded**. The brand belongs to Abdul Explorer — first Saudi to ski to the North Pole — so there's a specific cultural authority: Saudi pride married to world-class adventure credibility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark luxury: deep navy (#0a0e14 / #06090e) as dominant surface, bronze/gold (#aa6939) as the sole accent used sparingly, cream (#f5f5f0) for body text. Full-bleed hero photography with text overlays. Framer Motion whileInView reveals (fade + slide-up, staggered). Think editorial adventure magazine meets premium Saudi brand — NOT generic SaaS, NOT startup "we make it fun", NOT campy outdoors-store.

Anti-references: generic bootstrap travel sites, Airbnb Experiences clones, neon-on-dark "tech adventure" aesthetics.

### Design Principles
1. **Photography first** — imagery carries emotional weight; typography supports, never competes.
2. **Earn each word** — every heading and label is short and specific. No filler copy.
3. **RTL-native** — logical CSS properties everywhere (ms-/me-/ps-/pe-/start/end). All layouts must work in Arabic without code changes.
4. **Gold is rare** — bronze accent used only for interactive states, kickers, and key structural elements. Overuse kills it.
5. **Motion with purpose** — whileInView fade+slide on section entrances; hover micro-interactions on cards. No decorative animation loops.
